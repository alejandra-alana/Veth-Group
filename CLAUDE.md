# Veth Group — standing rules for Claude

## Cold email campaigns (Woodpecker, Instantly, any sequencer)
- **Every email step in every campaign must end with the full signature, the physical address, and a P.S.** No exceptions, including step 1 and every follow-up/bump.
  - Signature block: sender full name, title, company, street address, city/state/ZIP.
  - P.S.: an opt-out line, e.g. "P.S. If this isn't relevant right now, just let me know and I won't follow up" (use the client's P.S. if they gave one).
  - The client's sign-off (e.g. "Thanks, Victor") comes first, then the signature block, then the P.S.
  - Mailboxes usually have no signature saved, so put the block in the message body (`signature: NO_SIGNATURE`) rather than relying on `SENDER`.
- Before creating or editing a campaign, check every step against this rule; if a client's copy is missing the address or P.S., add it and flag it.
- Create campaigns as DRAFT; never start/run a campaign without explicit approval.
