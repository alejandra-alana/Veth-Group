# What I actually do day to day (Alejandra Alaña)

My title is Executive Assistant, but here's what I actually spend my days on. These come from the past few weeks of meetings and my open Monday tasks.

## 1. Miles's inbox and calendar (EA work)
- Run the daily 1-hour inbox session with Miles (now with Camilo too) and go through 100+ emails one by one
- Record the session, then use Claude to turn Miles's dictation into Superhuman drafts
- Review every draft for mistakes and send them (often 15+ a day), from Miles's account or my own
- Answer what I can from Miles's inbox on days we can't meet
- Schedule calls, rebook when Miles has conflicts, check the time slots offered in emails
- Cover calls for Miles when he's double-booked

## 2. Task capture and assignment (project management)
- After every call, put action items into Monday (with Claude)
- Assign tasks to Camilo and Jesús in Monday and ping them in Slack
- Decide who takes what, based on who's free and what's most urgent
- Follow up with the team so things get done before the deadline
- Run the Service Team Daily and walk the team through the Monday boards
- Keep the boards organized (Client CRM, New Campaign Launches, Non-Launch Tasks, Veth Group Leads)

## 3. Client account management
- Lead calls with smaller or simpler clients and join Miles on others
- Prepare before calls: read past email threads and bring questions
- After calls: send a same-day follow-up email and log next steps
- Send clients updates on what's happening internally
- Collect assets, lists, logins and approvals from clients
- Get copy approved by clients before launch
- Schedule kickoffs as soon as a new client signs, and join kickoffs
- Handle client requests: list changes, blacklists/DNC, new senders, lead routing

## 4. Campaign operations (outbound and LinkedIn)
- Request tech from Nathan at Complete.net (domains, inboxes, senders)
- Build and clean prospect lists with Claude Code (remove role emails, filter by region, etc.)
- Set up and run PostWatch campaigns and the Grok DM bot
- QA campaigns before they launch (especially Jesús's)
- Launch some campaigns myself and notify the client when they're live
- Track positives/replies (Positives Tracker)

## 5. Leads (sales support)
- Reply to leads Miles qualifies and book meetings with them
- Assign leads to Camilo in the Veth Group Leads board and track their status

## 6. Content and podcast support
- Coordinate with Profile Buddy (Chris) on revisions and client feedback
- Edit or coordinate clips and raw video from podcast/interview recordings
- Help with LinkedIn reconnects and Buffer issues
- Prep question lists for video shoots

## 7. Team ops and documentation
- Onboard and train new teammates (Camilo, tool access, inbox process)
- Get what's in Miles's head written down for the team
- Set up and improve workflows with Claude (Monday, Superhuman, Wispr Flow connectors)
- Help with internal projects (client health red/yellow/green, loyalty tiers, Pipedrive move)

## What isn't defined yet
- No written split between my role and Camilo's. We both work every account.
- No single owner for outbound campaigns or campaign QA
- No onboarding checklist beyond scheduling the kickoff
- No place that shows what services each client is getting right now
